# LinkedIn post

**Length:** 150–220 words. Under 1,300 characters if the user wants it to avoid "see more" fatigue; otherwise up to ~2,000.

## Structure (Hook → Story → Insight → Question)
1. **Hook (1–2 lines).** A claim or tension that makes someone stop. Example shape: "Anyone can build with AI now. That's not the hard part anymore."
2. **Story (2–3 short lines).** What you actually did, in one breath. Real, specific, no product pitch unless the user chose to name it.
3. **Insights (3–4 bullets).** Each starts with a bolded claim, then one clause. Draw from the project's real lessons: persistence, blockers as information, shipping as a system, AI vs human judgment.
4. **Close.** One question the reader can answer in the comments. Not a request for anything.
5. **Hashtags.** 3–4 maximum, at the end.

## Rules
- Reveal level follows the user's answer. If product-free, no product name, no links, no screenshots of it.
- If naming the product, put the link in the **last line**, not the first.
- Use `→` or short lines for scanning. No walls of text, no emoji strings.
- Pair with a title image (see `image-prompts.md`).
- Offer two variants when helpful: a short (≈120 words) and a standard one.
