## 🏗️ Phase 1: Pre-Release (Building Hype)
**Goal**: Build a waitlist, showcase your design strategy expertise, and inspire the community with how AI can elevate product building.
**Content Focus**: Behind-the-scenes, bridging the gap between design and productization, and the shift from Pixel Pusher to Product Strategist.

### Video Strategy (10–20 Second Clips)
*Format: Fast-paced, screen-recorded, high energy.*

- **Video 1: The "Strategist vs pixels" Flex**
  - **Hook (0-3s)**: "Stop just pushing pixels. Start bringing full product ideas to life with AI-assisted design."
  - **Body (3-15s)**: Flash rapid clips of the *design-audit-journey.html* timeline: Ideation, mapping heuristics, defining the architecture, and the final working Figma plugin. Emphasize how focusing on human experience design allowed the product to emerge.
  - **CTA (15-20s)**: "Dropping this LintAssist tomorrow to help you scale your design strategy. Comment 'LINT' and I'll DM you the link early."

- **Video 2: Elevate Your UX Strategy**
  - **Hook (0-3s)**: "What if you could consistently apply human experience design strategy without spending hours manually auditing every frame?"
  - **Body (3-15s)**: Show the Figma plugin side-by-side with a messy frame. Click "Analyze", fast-forward the loader, and boom—a beautiful, structured UI scorecard drops right onto the canvas. Show how it empowers the designer to focus on higher-level problem solving. 
  - **CTA (15-20s)**: "The ultimate tool for continuous design learning launches Thursday. Follow to see it live."

## 💬 The DM Strategy (Simple & Unique)

To beat the algorithm, you need engagement in the comments. We will use an automated or manual "DM for Link" strategy.

**The "Value Exchange" Tactic**:
1. **The Post/Tweet**: Publish a carousel or video showing a tear-down of a popular website using your tool. Include the actual generated scorecard. 
2. **The Ask**: "I'm giving away 50 free audit tokens today. Reply with a 🍎 (or any emoji) and I will DM you the secret access link."
3. **The DM Script**:
   > *"Hey! Thanks for the comment. Here is the link to the LintAssist plugin: [Link]. Your first 2 audits are entirely free (no account needed). Let me know what score your first frame gets!"*

**Why this works**:
- **Algorithm Boost**: 50 comments telling you they want the product signals to Twitter/IG that your post is highly valuable, pushing it to the Explore page/For You feed.
- **Personal Touch**: A direct DM from the creator builds immense trust. Asking them "what score they get" guarantees a follow-up conversation and likely a retained user.
