Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026
Short version: we collect the minimum needed to run the free/paid access system (your email, name, and a token) and to keep the product working. Your screenshots and audit reports are never stored — they're sent for one-time analysis and then gone. See below for the full detail.

What we collect

  • If you get a free or paid token (via the web app or the Figma plugin): your email address, and first/last name if you give it. Used to identify your account, apply your usage limit, and let you recover access on another device by re-entering the same email.
  • Your screenshot, when you run an audit: sent to an AI provider (see "Who we share data with" below) for that single request, to generate the report. Not stored by us before, during, or after — it exists only for the duration of that one request.
  • Usage metadata: which token made a request, roughly how much it cost us to run (token counts, not content), and a timestamp — used for our own cost tracking and abuse prevention, not shown to other users.
  • Payment information (paid plans, when live): handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details — only your subscription status.

What we don't collect

  • We don't store your screenshots or the generated audit report anywhere. Once a request completes, the image is gone. Use "Copy Report" (or the Figma "Place in Figma" action) to keep a copy yourself — we can't retrieve it for you later.
  • No tracking cookies, no analytics pixels, no ad networks.
  • We never ask for or store a password. Access is by a token, or by re-entering the email you signed up with.

Who we share data with

Your screenshot is sent to one of these providers to generate the audit, depending on availability — this is the only sharing that happens with every request:

  • OpenRouter (routing to Qwen or DeepSeek vision models) — our default, lowest-cost path.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — used only if the above is unavailable or fails.

Account and usage data lives in Cloudflare (Workers + D1 database), who host our backend. Payments, when live, are processed by Stripe. None of these providers use your data for anything beyond fulfilling your request — we don't sell or share data for marketing.

How long we keep it

Account records (email, name, token, plan) are kept until you ask us to delete them, or until the account is inactive long enough that we remove it as housekeeping. Screenshots and report content are never kept at all — there's nothing to delete there because nothing was stored.

Your choices

Email us (below) to have your account and its data deleted at any time. Since screenshots and reports were never stored, there's nothing further to remove on that front once a request completes.

Changes to this policy

If this changes in a way that matters — a new provider, new data we start collecting — we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions or a deletion request: reach out via the support contact listed on the web app or the Figma Community listing.
