## 🐦 20 Tweets: Design Strategy & AI Enablement

These tweets focus on the *why* and *how* of the product. They validate the designer's role, highlight how the plugin removes grunt work (WCAG, heuristic checks), and emphasize that AI is a tool to be validated by human expertise.

1. **The Core Philosophy**: Good UX strategy isn’t about pushing pixels faster. It’s about solving human problems. I built a tool to automate the compliance checks so you can focus on the empathy. 
2. **The ADA/WCAG Lifesaver**: Checking WCAG color contrast across 50 screens used to take me hours. Now my Figma plugin (powered by Claude 3.5) does it in 5 seconds. Work smarter, not harder.
3. **The "Start Here" Disclaimer**: AI is incredible, but it hallucinates. The LintAssist plugin I built gives you a 0-100 baseline score. Treat it as a starting point, then use your human visual design expertise to validate the fix.
4. **Behind the Scenes**: I didn't want to just mock up a tool; I wanted to build a real SaaS. Vibe coding with AI helped me bridge the gap from UX Designer to Product Builder in a single afternoon.
5. **The Heuristic Helper**: Do you always remember all 10 Nielsen UX heuristics when designing? Neither do I. That’s why I built an AI auditor that runs your frame against them instantly. 
6. **The Strategy Shift**: Stop letting basic spacing and contrast errors ruin your design reviews. Automate the baseline checks, and spend your meeting talking about user journey strategy instead.
7. **The Productization Journey**: Going from a Figma concept to a live SaaS with Stripe, Cloudflare, and a real database shifts your perspective. Designers who understand product architecture are the future.
8. **The Accessibility Advocate**: We talk a lot about accessible design (ADA compliance), but executing it consistently is hard. Let AI do the heavy lifting of flagging contrast and touch-target issues. 
9. **The Junior Designer Hack**: If you’re a junior designer, run your frames through LintAssist before sending them to your senior. It’s like having a real-time mentor flagging Gestalt principles.
10. **The Human Element**: Remember: My LintAssist doesn't replace you. It scans for basic design principles so you can focus on the complex, messy, beautiful human experience. 
11. **The Consistency Key**: Consistent UX wins trust. The hardest part of a massive design system is maintaining that consistency. An automated AI audit pass is the best safety net a team can have.
12. **The "Why"**: I built this because I was tired of second-guessing my visual hierarchy. Now I get a quantitative score on my layout before I ever present it to a stakeholder. 
13. **The Validation Loop**: Design -> AI Audit -> Validate with Expertise -> Refine. This is the new fast-track workflow for modern UX strategy. 
14. **The Cognitive Load Reduction**: Designing complex dashboards takes immense cognitive load. Offload the ADA contrast checking to AI. Save your brainpower for the information architecture.
15. **The Transparency Post**: How my plugin works: It exports your Figma frame directly to Claude Vision. Claude checks it against 8 UX frameworks. But *always* verify the output. AI makes mistakes; you are the strategist.
16. **The Developer Handoff**: Imagine handing over designs to devs with a pre-validated WCAG and Accessibility scorecard attached. Handoffs just got 10x smoother.
17. **The Product Builder Flex**: Designing a slick UI is fun. Building the backend database and Stripe integration to actually sell it? That makes you a true product owner.
18. **The Gestalt Check**: "Does this layout group related items effectively?" Our brains process Gestalt principles instantly, but AI can help catch what our fatigued eyes miss at 4 PM on a Friday.
19. **The Empathy Angle**: Accessibility isn't a checklist; it's empathy. But checklists help enforce it. The LintAssist plugin makes sure you don't forget the baseline ADA requirements.
20. **The Launch Call**: The LintAssist plugin is officially live! Run it on your Figma frames to catch visual hierarchy and contrast errors instantly. (Link in bio. First 2 audits are free!)
