# Product video scripts

Three short vertical videos (9:16, 30–40 seconds each), each in a different storytelling format. All three show the same product truthfully: a fast, honest second opinion on a screen, on the web, in Figma and in Chrome.

## Facts every script may use (all true today)
- Two free audits with no account. Then five a day with just an email.
- Upload a screenshot (web), select a frame (Figma), or capture the visible page (Chrome).
- Returns a score and ranked findings, each with an observation and a concrete next step.
- Starts with Nielsen's heuristics, Gestalt and accessibility. Other lenses are optional.
- One token works on web, Figma and Chrome.
- In Figma the report can be placed on the canvas next to the frame. Reports can be copied.
- Screenshots and reports are not stored.
- Positioning: a second opinion, not the final word.

**Do not claim:** user counts, installs, audit counts, time saved as a number, compliance certification, or that it replaces a designer or an accessibility audit.

## How to produce these

**Use real screen recordings for anything showing the product.** AI video generators garble interface text and invent UI. Record the actual web app, the Figma plugin and the Chrome side panel, then use the generator only for atmosphere, b-roll and the emotional "struggle" scenes.

**Shared look for generated scenes (prepend to every generator prompt):**
> Cinematic, warm and calm. Cream and deep espresso tones with a single burnt-orange accent. Soft natural light, shallow depth of field, slow gentle camera movement. Over-the-shoulder and close-up shots of a designer's workspace. No readable text, no logos, no screens with legible UI. Vertical 9:16.

**On-screen text:** add it in the editor, not in the generator. Large, centered, 1 short line per scene, high contrast.

**Voice:** calm, first person or direct address, not salesy. Aim for about 2.5 words per second, so 30 seconds is roughly 75 words.

# Shot list for recording the real product (do this once, reuse in all three)

1. **Web:** drag a screenshot into the app. Capture the full flow: upload, audit running, score ring, findings.
2. **Figma:** select a frame, run the plugin, show the report placed on the canvas next to the frame.
3. **Chrome:** open the side panel on a live page, capture the visible page, show the result.
4. **Close-ups:** score ring filling, a finding highlighted, the "copy report" action.
5. **End card:** logo, `lintassist.com`, "Two free audits. No account."

Record at 1080×1920 (vertical) or crop from 4K. Use a clean demo screen with a few obvious, believable issues (an easy-to-miss primary button, faint secondary text, items spaced oddly), so the findings are credible.

# Variations to test
- **Hook swaps:** "Same review comment, again?" · "Your design looks perfect until someone else sees it." · "Stop spending hours checking what a second opinion can catch in seconds."
- **Lengths:** cut each to a 15-second version using only hook, mechanism and CTA.
- **Platform notes:** LinkedIn favors the transformation angle (Video 3) and the context/tension story (Video 2); short-form feeds favor Video 1's character arc.
